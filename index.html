Questions, answered

Good to know before you enquire.

Is this the official Apna College website?

No. This is an independently operated course information and inquiry website. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by Apna College. Use the official site for enrollment, final course details, terms and support.

Does this include every batch Apna College has ever offered?

This index includes the 47 course and batch pages found in Apna College’s public sitemap during the Nov 1, 2026 review. Removed, private, unlisted or pre-sitemap pages may be missing, so this cannot be certified as a complete record of every batch ever sold. Similar alternate URLs for the same cohort are grouped together rather than counted twice.

Are these batches currently open for enrollment?

Availability could not be confirmed from the dates displayed on the public course pages checked on Nov 1, 2026; the listed cohort dates have passed. Check the official listing or contact Apna College for a current intake date.

Does the WhatsApp button unlock a course?

No. It opens an inquiry to the site operator only. It does not provide, unlock, share or sell course content. Enrollment and access remain subject to the official provider's terms and any written authorization.

Are ₹399 and ₹599 official prices?

No. These are proposed promotional-price concepts supplied for this guide. No payment is accepted here. Do not present them as an offer or take payment unless resale or bundle rights and the buyer's exact access rights are confirmed in writing.

Why don't the course cards use official thumbnails?

Course names and public syllabus summaries link to the official pages. Thumbnail reuse permission could not be verified, so this guide uses original typographic card art instead of copying course images.

Are jobs, placements or income guaranteed?

No. Learning can help build technical skills and project experience, but employment, internship, freelance income and placement outcomes are not guaranteed and depend on many factors.
